Our Investment Philosophy

Finding Unrecognized Value

We seek value amongst small- and medium-sized companies that are widely misunderstood, ignored and/or underfollowed. We want to own durable franchises with significant barriers-to-entry led by skilled management teams when their shares are mispriced.

Waiting for the Perfect Pitch

First, we find the businesses we want to own and then we patiently wait for the right entry price—one with an ample margin of safety.* Years of diligent research often culminate in a moment of action. Since value can be fleeting, we must be ready to pounce. By exploiting the dislocations that arise from short-term thinking, we drive long-term returns. We aim to arbitrage time.

Mitigating Risk

Maximizing shareholder returns is more than just math. We integrate all material issues, including sustainability-related factors, as part of the broader review of material risks and opportunities for any investment. These considerations are incorporated qualitatively in our bottom-up research, and quantitatively in our financial models—with proprietary Business Resilience Risk ratings assigned to every holding.

Looking Over the Horizon

Getting swept up in the euphoria or misery of the market can lead to emotional decisions and bad outcomes. Our job is to drown out the noise and think long-term. As disciplined, value investors, we look past today’s turbulence to see tomorrow’s possibilities.

*Attempting to purchase a stock with a margin of safety does not protect investors from the loss of their investment, volatility associated with stocks, declining fundamentals, external forces, or our incorrect assumptions.

Our contrarian views result from meticulous, fundamental bottom-up research.

Our Investment Process

  • Investment universe clearly defined
  • Analysts specialize
  • Proprietary watchlist honed
  • Portfolio company executives queried

  • Business model and valuation studied
  • Investment hypothesis established
  • Devil’s advocate consulted
  • Sustainability-related issues identified
  • Preliminary report generated

  • Industry assessed/reassessed
  • Company scrutinized
  • Valuation models created/updated
  • Proprietary debt, business resilience risk and moat rating assigned/reviewed
  • Company management questioned
  • Comprehensive research report created

  • Investment thesis debated
  • Critical issues considered
  • Downside risk contemplated and quantified
  • Additional channel checks conducted

These portfolio construction practices may vary and are subject to change.

The Russell 2500™ Value Index measures the performance of the small to mid-cap value segment of the U.S. equity universe.
